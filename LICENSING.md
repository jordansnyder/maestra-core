## Who can use Maestra Core for free

You may use, modify, and self-host Maestra Core **at no cost** for:

- **Students and educators** — coursework, teaching, academic projects.
- **Research and non-commercial** use — labs, artists, non-profits, hobbyists.
- **Any non-production** use — evaluation, development, testing, demos.

## Who needs a commercial license

Any **for-profit entity** running Maestra Core in **production** needs a
commercial license. This is what funds ongoing development.

Larger and multi-site installations (e.g. the Maestra Cloud Gateway federating
sites across locations) are the typical commercial tier.

**To purchase or ask about a commercial license: licensing@maestra.cc**

## Why the SDKs are MIT

The SDKs are how your tools *connect* to Maestra. We keep them MIT so anyone can
integrate Maestra into any project — commercial or not — with zero friction. The
commercial terms apply to the **platform**, not the client libraries.
