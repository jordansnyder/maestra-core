# Contributing to Maestra

Thanks for your interest in contributing! A few things to know first.

## Licensing of contributions

Maestra Core is offered under the [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE), and we
also sell commercial licenses to fund development (see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)).
To keep that possible, **all contributors must agree to our Contributor License
Agreement** before we can merge their work.

- Read [CLA.md](CLA.md).
- By opening a pull request, you confirm that you have read the CLA and that your
  contribution is made under its terms.
- For your first PR, a maintainer will ask you to confirm agreement (a comment on
  the PR is sufficient until we automate CLA sign-off).

The CLA lets us (the Licensor: Jordan Snyder and Aaron Geiser) license the
project under both BSL and commercial terms. You keep the copyright to your work.

## Development

- See [README.md](README.md) and [PIPELINE.md](PIPELINE.md) for architecture and
  conventions.
- SDKs live in `sdks/` and are MIT-licensed; the core platform is BSL.
- Run the relevant SDK tests before submitting (e.g. `pytest` in `sdks/python`,
  `npm test` in `sdks/js`).

## Questions

Open a GitHub Discussion, or email licensing@maestra.cc for licensing matters.
